NIMBORA Softphone turns a computer, a phone or a browser tab into an [enterprise PBX](/integrations/telephony/enterprise-pbx) extension. One app runs on every platform and carries the platform's name, so it is white-label too.

## What it does

- Places and receives calls on the PBX. Signaling is SIP over a secure WebSocket; audio is WebRTC, encrypted with SRTP. It rings, shows a notification, and displays the caller's number or name.
- During a call: mute, hold, a keypad for menus (DTMF), **transfer** (directly, or after talking to the colleague first), a **three-way conference**, and a call timer. A second caller hears busy.
- **Recents**: the extension's calls from the PBX's call log, missed calls in red.
- **Contacts**: one directory with colleagues and their **presence**, call groups and queues, and the company's **customers** (see below), with accent-insensitive search.
- **My presence**: Available, Away or Offline, with a message colleagues see (« En réunion jusqu'à 15 h »).
- **Voicemail** in one tap. It dials `*98`.
- **Do-not-disturb** and **forward all calls**, from the app.
- **Calls handed over by the AI** come with the AI agent's handoff card (see below).
- Audio output selection, physical keyboard on computers, and light or dark theme.

## Transfer and three-way conference

**Transférer** offers two choices. The colleagues who are available right now are suggested.

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Appeler d'abord** | The customer is put on hold and the app calls the colleague. You talk to them first. |
| **Transférer directement** | The PBX calls the colleague for the customer at once (blind transfer). |

While you consult a colleague:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Basculer** | Talk to the other one: the customer or the colleague. The other one is on hold. |
| **Transférer** | The PBX connects the customer and the colleague, and you leave (attended transfer, SIP REFER with Replaces). If the colleague has not answered yet, the PBX calls them for the customer. |
| **Conférence** | The three of you hear each other. The microphone button mutes you on both calls. **Se retirer** leaves the customer and the colleague together; **Tout raccrocher** ends both calls. |
| **Annuler** or the red button | Hang up on the colleague and go back to the customer. |

If the customer hangs up during the consultation, the colleague's call goes on as a normal call. The conference is mixed in the app with WebAudio, so it needs nothing on the PBX.

Each colleague shows one state, refreshed every 20 seconds while Contacts is open:

| State | When |
|---|---|
| **Disponible** | Ready to take a call |
| **En appel** / **Sonne** | Talking or holding a call / a call is ringing or being placed |
| **Ne pas déranger** | Do-not-disturb is on |
| **Absent** | The person chose it, with an optional message |
| **Hors ligne** | No phone or app connected, or the person chose « Hors ligne » |

On a call, ringing and do-not-disturb follow the extension by themselves. Available, Away and Offline are chosen in **Réglages → Ma présence**. The same presence appears in the console, in the **Postes** tab of the PBX page.

Below the colleagues, Contacts lists the **customers** from customer memory: the most recent ones, or every customer matching the search (name or number, searched on the server). VIP customers carry a badge, with their usual advisor when known. An incoming call from a known customer shows their name. Customers are not listed when customer memory is off.

Presence comes from the PBX's presence service. It needs the rights `chatd.users.presences.read` and `chatd.users.*.presences.update`, included in the service account's policy since this version:

- **PBX shared by the operator**: run `create_service_account.py --provisioner` again on the operator's PBX, then **Activer la téléphonie** again for each company (or the operator's « Provisionner »). Each company's policy is then updated in place.
- **Your own PBX**: run `deploy/pbx/scripts/create_service_account.py` again.

Until then, the app still works and shows only who is on a call.

## Calls handed over by the AI

When an AI agent transfers a caller to a PBX queue or extension, the app shows the agent's **handoff card** as soon as the call rings, and keeps it during the call. The card holds:

- the reason for the call and a three-sentence summary;
- the customer's mood: satisfied, neutral, annoyed or angry;
- the suggested next step;
- the data the AI collected, such as a budget, an address or a date;
- the conversation with the AI, on demand.

The agent never asks the customer to start over. Answering marks the card as taken. The card is matched by the caller's number, presented nationally or internationally, among the cards of the last 10 minutes. Version 1.1.0 or later of the app is needed.

## Pairing a device

1. In the console, open **Téléphonie d'entreprise → Postes** and click **Softphone** on the person's extension.
2. A **one-time code** appears, with a **QR code**. The code is valid for 15 minutes.
3. On the device, scan the QR code: its `nimbora-softphone://` link opens the app already filled in. Or open the app and type the platform address and the code.

The extension needs a **browser (WebRTC) line**. Extensions created with **Ligne : navigateur** have one. A desk-phone line is refused with an explanation.

## Security

- **A code works once.** It expires after 15 minutes. After 10 wrong attempts from one address in 10 minutes, that address is blocked for 10 minutes.
- **Device token**: pairing returns a token that the app keeps on the device. The platform stores only its SHA-256 hash.
- **SIP secret**: neither the platform nor the device stores it. The app reads it from the PBX each time it starts, over HTTPS with its device token, and keeps it in memory. When an administrator changes the line's secret (**Postes → SIP** in the console, or on the PBX), the app reads its account again as soon as the PBX refuses the old secret, and restarts the line with the new one, without pairing again. It does so once per refusal and never during a call (version 1.2.1).
- **Revoking**: **Postes → Softphones** lists the paired devices with their platform, version and last connection. **Révoquer** cuts a device off at once. **Déconnecter cet appareil** in the app does the same. Deleting an extension revokes all its devices.
- STUN and TURN servers come from the platform's configuration. The TURN credentials expire.

## Connection to the PBX

By default the app connects to `wss://<PBX host>/api/asterisk/ws`, the engine's WebSocket. Its SIP domain is the PBX host. If the PBX is behind another name or a proxy, set **Connexion des softphones au PBX** in **Postes → Softphones**.

The PBX must have a valid TLS certificate for its WebSocket address, because browsers and apps refuse self-signed ones.

## Platforms and downloads

| Platform | Package |
|---|---|
| Windows | `.msi` or `.exe` installer |
| macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) | `.dmg`; signed and notarized when the Apple secrets are set in CI |
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.AppImage` |
| Android | `.apk` |
| iPhone / iPad | Simulator build in CI; TestFlight / App Store needs an Apple certificate |
| Browser | Installable web app (PWA), from `softphone/dist` |

The app lives in [`softphone/`](https://github.com/optimiumnexusllc/nimbora/tree/main/softphone): a React and JsSIP web app inside a Tauri 2 shell. The `NIMBORA Softphone` workflow builds every platform. Pushing a tag `softphone-vX.Y.Z` drafts a release with all the packages.

## API

The app uses `/api/v1/softphone/*` with `Authorization: Bearer <device token>`:

| Method | Path | |
|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/softphone/activate` | `{code, device_name, platform, app_version}` → `{token, config}` |
| `GET` | `/softphone/config` | SIP account, WebSocket, domain, ICE servers, brand |
| `GET` | `/softphone/history` | Recent calls of the extension |
| `GET` | `/softphone/directory?q=…` | Colleagues with presence, groups, queues, customers (`q` searches customers; `customers: null` when customer memory is off) |
| `PUT` | `/softphone/presence` | `{state: available \| away \| offline, status}` |
| `GET` / `PUT` | `/softphone/status` | Do-not-disturb, forward all calls |
| `GET` | `/softphone/handoff?number=…` | The AI's handoff card for the call ringing now (`{card: … \| null}`) |
| `POST` | `/softphone/handoff/{id}/answered` | The person took the call |
| `DELETE` | `/softphone/device` | Sign out (revoke this device) |

## Limits

- **Mobile calls with the app closed** need VoIP push notifications (PushKit on iOS, FCM on Android), relayed by the platform. This is planned. Until then, calls ring while the app is open; on computers it can stay minimized.
- One customer call at a time, plus the colleague consulted during a transfer. The three-way conference is mixed in the app: the employee's connection carries both calls, and when they leave (**Se retirer**) the PBX keeps the two others connected.
