**Supervision** in the sidebar shows what is happening right now, in one picture: AI calls, the built-in contact center and the enterprise PBX.

The console is **real time**: the server pushes changes over a WebSocket as they happen (a caller joins a queue, an agent answers or pauses, an AI call starts or ends). The header reads **En direct · temps réel**. If WebSockets are blocked on the way (a proxy without WebSocket support), the console falls back to refreshing every 5 seconds and says so.

## AI calls: follow and steer

Each AI call card shows:

- the agent and the caller;
- the duration;
- who is speaking;
- the detected language;
- the current step;
- the last sentences.

Open a card to follow the conversation **word by word**, with tool calls. You can then:

- **Souffler à l'IA** (whisper to the AI): add an instruction the caller does not hear. For example, "offer a 10% discount" or "suggest a callback tomorrow". It applies from the AI's next reply and stays in force for the rest of the call, across steps.
- **Faire dire** (make it say): the agent says your sentence right away.
- **Transférer à un conseiller** (transfer to an agent): hand the caller to a queue, built-in or **of the enterprise PBX**. This works like the agent's own transfer tool, so the handoff card is included.
- **Raccrocher** (hang up): end the call, optionally after a last sentence.

Every action is written into the call's feed.

<Note>
Calls run on whichever server picked them up. Their live feed and the supervisors' commands go through Redis, so the console works with any number of API and worker instances.
</Note>

## Human agents: listen, whisper, barge

The agents table shows each agent's state: available, ringing, on a call (with whom and for how long), paused (with the reason) or offline. Queues show the callers waiting, the longest wait, the calls in progress and the available agents.

Seats marked **Superviseur** (**Centre d'appels → Postes**) can supervise an agent who is on a call. NIMBORA rings the supervisor's own softphone, and once they answer:

- **Écouter** (listen): hear the call without being heard;
- **Chuchoter** (whisper): talk to the agent only, while the customer hears nothing;
- **Intervenir** (barge): join the conversation.

During the session, keys **4 / 5 / 6** switch between listen, whisper and barge.

Supervision runs `ChanSpy` in a dialplan context that only the API can reach. It is limited to one agent of the same organization.

## Enterprise PBX

When an [enterprise PBX](/integrations/telephony/enterprise-pbx) is connected, the console adds its live state.

- **PBX queues**: callers waiting, by name or number, with their wait time. The longest wait turns red past the service-level threshold. Each queue also shows its agents available, on a call and paused.
- **PBX agents**: each agent's state, the call in progress (who, which direction, how long, on hold), and pause and resume buttons.
- **Other conversations**: calls of extensions that are not queue agents (reception, management, sales…).
- **Callers sent by the AI** carry an **IA** mark in the queue (hover it for the reason and the summary). Once someone answers, the reason and the customer's mood appear under the agent's call. See [AI agent → PBX queue](/integrations/telephony/enterprise-pbx).

### Listen, whisper, barge on the PBX

Choose **Votre poste** (your extension) at the top of the PBX block. NIMBORA preselects the extension whose e-mail matches your account. Then click **Écouter**, **Chuchoter** or **Intervenir** on a call. Your phone rings; once you answer, you are in the call.

Listening is **off by default**. **Réglages de l'écoute** (listening settings) turns it on, and only after you confirm that staff have been informed. The settings choose:

- the modes allowed (listen, whisper, barge);
- the supervisor extensions (none ticked: any extension);
- the extensions that are **never** listened to, such as management, HR or staff representatives;
- the service-level threshold in seconds.

Every session is written to the **Journal des écoutes** (listening log): who listened to whom, when and how.

<Warning>
Listening to calls is regulated. Labour and data-protection laws usually require informing staff beforehand and limiting the purpose to training or quality. Some countries also require informing callers. Check the rules that apply to you before turning it on.
</Warning>

How it works:

1. The PBX's call API rings the supervisor's phone (`POST /calls`).
2. Once answered, the call enters the `nimbora-supervision` dialplan context, which the PBX kit installs (`deploy/pbx/templates/nimbora-supervision.conf`). No phone can dial this context.
3. The context checks the target line and the options again, then runs `ChanSpy` on that one line: `PJSIP/<line>-`, where the trailing dash means a single line only.

Waiting callers come from the AMI `QueueStatus` action through `wazo-amid`, always asked about one named queue of the company. The engine's REST APIs do not list waiting callers.

### Alerts

**Alertes** (next to the listening settings) watches the queues so that nobody waits unnoticed. Three rules:

| Rule | Raised when |
|---|---|
| Long wait | a caller has waited longer than the threshold you set (critical past twice the threshold) |
| Queue full | more callers are waiting in one queue than the threshold you set |
| No agent | callers are waiting and no agent of that queue is available, or none is logged in |

You can watch every queue or a few of them. Alerts are:

- **on the console**: red and orange banners at the top of **Supervision** and on the wall display. With **Recevoir les alertes dans le navigateur** (receive alerts in the browser), a browser notification also pops up when an alert starts, even in another tab;
- **sent to people who are not looking**: a **webhook** such as Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat or n8n, which receives `{"text": "…", "alert": {…}}`, and/or **e-mail** to the recipients you list. E-mail goes out through one of your agents' e-mail tools (a **Message** tool, SMTP, with its sender).

A message goes out when an alert starts and when it ends (**Rétabli**, with how long it lasted). It is never sent twice within the delay you choose, so a queue going back and forth does not flood the channel. **Enregistrer et tester** (save and test) sends a test message. The **Journal** tab keeps the latest alerts and their ends.

The worker checks every minute. The console reacts at once, on every live update.

### Wall display

**Mur d'affichage** (wall display, at `/supervision/wall`) is made for a TV on the call-center floor. It shows, large and readable from across the room:

- callers waiting and the longest wait;
- agents available and on a call;
- AI calls;
- one tile per queue;
- each agent's state;
- the active alerts.

It updates in real time and has a full-screen button.

### Statistics

**Statistiques des files et des conseillers** (queue and agent statistics) shows, for today, yesterday, 7 or 30 days:

- received, answered and abandoned calls;
- the answered rate and the **service level** (calls answered within the threshold);
- the average wait;
- per agent: calls answered, average talk time, time logged in, time paused and occupancy.

Days follow the company's time zone (**Mon entreprise**).

### Requirements on the PBX

- The service account needs `calld.calls.create`, `amid.action.QueueStatus.create`, `call-logd.queues.statistics.read` and `call-logd.agents.statistics.read`. They are part of `deploy/pbx/integration-policy.json` (110 ACL). Re-run `scripts/create_service_account.py` after upgrading.
- The PBX kit installs the supervision dialplan. On a PBX installed another way, copy `nimbora-supervision.conf` to `/etc/asterisk/extensions_extra.d/` and run `asterisk -rx "dialplan reload"`.
- For real time, the PBX must send call and agent events. On **Téléphonie d'entreprise**, click **Recevoir les événements**; if the PBX was connected before this version, click **Événements reçus** once more to subscribe to the new events. Without them, the console still refreshes the PBX every 10 seconds.

## API

| Endpoint | |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/supervision` | Snapshot: AI calls, agents, queues, KPIs |
| `GET /api/v1/supervision/calls/{run_id}/events?after=n` | Live feed of an AI call |
| `POST /api/v1/supervision/calls/{run_id}/actions` | `instruct`, `say`, `takeover`, `hangup` |
| `POST /api/v1/supervision/spy` | Listen / whisper / barge on an agent |
| `PUT /api/v1/contact-center/me/presence` | Available / paused (with reason) / offline |
| `WS /api/v1/supervision/stream?token=…` | Real time: `{"type": "ai" \| "pbx", "data": …}` when something changes; send `{"type": "refresh"}` to force one |
| `GET /api/v1/pbx/supervision/live` | PBX queues (waiting callers), agents, conversations |
| `POST /api/v1/pbx/supervision/spy` | Listen / whisper / barge on a PBX extension (`supervisor_user_uuid`, `target_user_uuid`, `mode`) |
| `GET/PUT /api/v1/pbx/supervision/settings` | Listening on/off, modes, supervisors, protected extensions, service-level threshold |
| `GET /api/v1/pbx/supervision/sessions` | Listening log |
| `GET/PUT /api/v1/pbx/supervision/alerts` | Alert rules and channels, the company's e-mail tools, the latest alerts |
| `POST /api/v1/pbx/supervision/alerts/test` | Send a test message on the configured channels |
| `GET /api/v1/pbx/supervision/statistics?period=today\|yesterday\|7d\|30d` | Queue and agent statistics |
