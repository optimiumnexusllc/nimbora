NIMBORA runs AI agents and a human team side by side, on the platform's own telephony (Asterisk, deployed by `deploy/enterprise`).

## Queues and seats

- **Queues** (`support`, `ventes`…) distribute calls to their members with a strategy, a ring timeout, a maximum wait and an overflow destination.
- **Seats** are softphone extensions. Each agent answers in the browser (**Centre d'appels → Mon softphone**) with no SIP client to install. Credentials are shown once.
- Only **available** agents are rung. With **Pause** in the softphone (pick a reason: lunch, training…), the agent leaves every queue until they resume. The supervision console shows the pause too.

## From the AI to a person

When an AI agent transfers to `queue:<slug>`, the agent who answers sees a **handoff card** on their softphone. It contains:

- the reason;
- a summary of the conversation;
- the customer's name and mood;
- the suggested next step;
- the data collected and the transcript.

## Callbacks

See [Schedule Callback](/voice-agent/tools/schedule-callback). Callers who leave a queue unanswered become callbacks automatically. **Rappels** lists them, and one click calls the customer back.
