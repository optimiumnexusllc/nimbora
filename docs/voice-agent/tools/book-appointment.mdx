---

## What the agent does

1. **find_slots**: asks the calendar for free slots, optionally on a given day or after a given time. It reads two or three of them out ("mardi 24 novembre à 10h").
2. **book**: books the slot the caller picks. The slot is checked again at that moment, because someone may have just taken it.
3. **my_appointments** / **cancel**: tells the caller about their upcoming appointments and cancels one. Callers can only cancel appointments booked from their own number.

The agent never invents availability. Two calls booking the same slot at the same second cannot both succeed: the second caller is offered another slot.

## Calendars

| Calendar | Setup |
|---|---|
| **NIMBORA agenda** | Nothing to configure. Bookings appear in **Activité → Rendez-vous**. |
| **CalDAV** | Nextcloud, iCloud, Zimbra, SOGo, Radicale, Fastmail… Enter the calendar collection URL and a *Basic Auth* credential (user + app password). Recurring events are expanded by the server. |
| **Google Calendar** | Create a service account in Google Cloud, download its JSON key, then add it as a *Google service account* credential. Share the calendar with the service account e-mail ("Make changes to events"). Enter the calendar ID. |

Whatever the calendar, every booking is also listed in **Activité → Rendez-vous**. You can cancel it there; that also removes it from CalDAV or Google.

## Opening hours and rules

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Opening hours | Monday–Friday, 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00 |
| Slot length | 30 min |
| Buffer between appointments | 0 min |
| Minimum notice | 60 min |
| Booking horizon | 30 days |
| Time zone | The organization's |

Hours are read in the calendar's time zone, so daylight saving time never shifts "09:00". Transparent ("free") and cancelled events do not block slots.

**Test the calendar** in the tool editor connects with the saved settings and shows the next free slots.
