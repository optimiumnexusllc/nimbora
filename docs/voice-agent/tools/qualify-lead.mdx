---
title: "Qualify Lead"
description: "The agent records each prospect's need, budget, timeline and decision power during the call; the team follows the leads through the sales pipeline."
---

With a **Qualify Lead** tool, the agent turns an interested caller into a lead on the **Prospects** page. During the call it records:
- the need;
- the budget, from and to;
- the timeline;
- whether the caller decides alone;
- what interests them;
- their name, e-mail or company.

It can record these one at a time, as the conversation goes.

## Score and next question

Each answer adds to a score out of 100:

| Known | Points |
|---|---|
| Need | 25 |
| Budget | 25 |
| Timeline | 20 |
| Decides alone (with others: half) | 15 |
| Name or e-mail | 10 |
| Interests | 5 |

After each save, the tool tells the agent what is **still unknown**, so the agent asks the next useful question instead of reading out a form. Once everything is known, the tool suggests the next step: a visit, a quote or an appointment.

When a new lead reaches the tool's threshold (60 by default), it moves to **Qualifié** by itself. On the Prospects page, leads at 70 or more show as « Chaud », from 40 as « Tiède », and below that as « Froid ».

## One lead per prospect

The next call from the same number completes the **open** lead (Nouveau to Négociation) instead of creating another one. A lead marked won or lost stays closed: a new project from that caller starts a new lead. A web chat without a phone number keeps one lead per conversation. When customer memory knows the caller, the lead is linked to their customer profile.

## Pipeline

**Prospects** shows six columns: **Nouveau → Qualifié → Proposition → Négociation → Gagné / Perdu**. Each column shows its number of leads and the total of their maximum budgets. Change a lead's stage from its card; marking it lost asks why. Open a card to edit it, set an owner and a next action, or add notes. **Exporter** downloads every lead as CSV.

The tool's settings are the currency budgets are given in (XOF by default) and the score from which a new lead becomes « Qualifié ».

Combine it with [Search Catalog](/voice-agent/tools/search-catalog): the agent searches the items that fit the budget, then records the ones the caller liked as interests.

## API

| Method | Path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/api/v1/leads?stage=&q=` | Leads, best score first, and the pipeline |
| `POST` / `PUT` / `DELETE` | `/api/v1/leads[/{id}]` | Add, change, delete |
| `POST` | `/api/v1/leads/{id}/stage` | `{stage, lost_reason}`; won and lost close the lead |
| `GET` | `/api/v1/leads/export.csv` | CSV export |
