title: "Run NIMBORA as a SaaS"
description: "Plans, quotas, AI and human minutes, shared PBX and monthly usage for an operator hosting many companies."
---

With `SAAS_MODE=true`, one NIMBORA installation hosts many client companies. Each company gets a subscription on a plan; the operator manages companies, plans and invoicing from the super-admin console (**Clients et abonnements**).

## Settings

| Variable | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `SAAS_MODE` | `true` to enable plans, quotas and the operator console |
| `SAAS_DEFAULT_PLAN` | plan of a new company (default `starter`) |
| `SAAS_TRIAL_DAYS` | free trial length (default 14) |
| `SAAS_GRACE_DAYS` | days of calls after a failed payment (default 3) |
| `SAAS_PLATFORM_NAME` | name shown to companies |
| `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` | online payment (optional) |
| `ENABLE_SIGNUP` | `false` to create accounts only by invitation (the sign-up page then shows "Inscriptions fermées") |
| `PBX_PROVISIONING_*` | the operator's shared PBX, see [Enterprise PBX](/integrations/telephony/enterprise-pbx#shared-pbx-of-the-platform-one-tenant-per-company) |

## What a plan contains

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly price | excluding tax |
| AI minutes included, then price per minute | calls answered or placed by AI agents. A trial that has used its minutes stops calling; an active subscription is billed for the overage |
| **Human minutes included, then price per minute** | answered calls between a customer and an employee on the enterprise PBX. Counted apart from AI minutes, never blocked, billed for the overage |
| Concurrent calls, AI agents, campaigns | limits |
| Human seats | agents of the built-in contact center **plus** extensions of the PBX |
| Options | local AI, contact center, campaigns, API, white label, custom voices, **platform PBX** (`pbx_shared`), **own PBX** (`pbx_dedicated`) |

An empty value means unlimited. Each company can get its own limits (overrides) from its **Gérer** dialog.

Default plans (editable, never overwritten on restart):

| Plan | Price | AI minutes | Human minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 99 € | 1 000, then 0.12 € | 1 000, then 0.03 € |
| Business | 399 € | 5 000, then 0.09 € | 10 000, then 0.02 € |
| Enterprise | 1 499 € | 25 000, then 0.06 € | 50 000, then 0.01 € |

## How human minutes are counted

A human call is billed when it was **answered**, has a **customer** on the other end (an inbound or outbound call, not an internal one between two extensions) and was **not taken by an AI agent** (those are AI minutes). The billed time is the duration in the PBX call log, from answer to hang-up.

Calls are counted as they end, from the PBX's call-log event, and an **hourly catch-up** reads the PBX call logs since the last one seen, so a call whose event was lost during an outage is still counted. Each call log is counted only once.

Companies see their human minutes on their **Abonnement** page, next to their AI minutes.

## Monthly usage and invoicing

**Clients et abonnements → Consommation du mois** lists, for a calendar month, every company's AI minutes and human minutes against its plan (with its overrides) and the overage amount. **Exporter (CSV)** downloads the same table (semicolon-separated, opens in Excel) to prepare invoices.

The same data is available from the API: `GET /api/v1/saas/admin/usage?month=2026-11` (`&format=csv` for the file).
